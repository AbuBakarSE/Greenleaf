FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Here are the top questions our clients ask before getting started.

How does long a typical carbon footprint assessment take?

A typical carbon footprint assessment usually takes 2-4 weeks, depending on the size of your business and data availability. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your operations.

What industry do you work with?

We work with businesses across all industries including manufacturing, retail, technology, hospitality, and professional services. Our approach is tailored to your specific sector.

What if we already have some data and reports?

Perfect! We can build on your existing data and reports. This often speeds up the process and ensures continuity in your sustainability journey.

Do we even have the internal capability to pull this off?

That's exactly why we're here! We provide the expertise, tools, and guidance you need. You don't need to be sustainability experts – we'll walk you through every step.

What if the rules change or targets become impossible to meet?

We stay on top of regulatory changes and help you adapt your strategy accordingly. Our approach is flexible and designed to evolve with changing requirements and market conditions.
